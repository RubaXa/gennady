# FORMAT1: interpretation boundaries and next experiments

These notes do not change the frozen trial plan, variants, order, budgets, or acceptance.

## Research synthesis

Pseudocode instructions, action–observation examples, and executable code as an action space
are different interventions. Published gains on NLP/logic tasks are not proof of savings for a
Flash repository agent; published negative results require keeping the prose control.

FORMAT1 compares prose, algorithmic notation, and action–observation within the same module
fill procedure. Its representation contrast is prose versus pseudocode/observation. Current
versus prose additionally changes organization and is not a pure representation comparison.
